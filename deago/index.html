📜 Command & Conduct Policy

  1. All Division Commanders report directly to Shadow Op.
  2. Division Commanders manage routine approved activities.
  3. Major projects and leadership appointments require founder approval.
  4. Promotions require documented performance and authorization.
  5. Members must respect privacy, honesty, confidentiality, and applicable law.
  6. Members may report misconduct directly to Shadow Op.
  7. No member may impersonate law enforcement or claim government authority.
  8. No unauthorized surveillance, access, harassment, or interference with investigations is permitted.
