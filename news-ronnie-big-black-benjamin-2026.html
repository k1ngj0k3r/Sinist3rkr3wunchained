Ronnie “Big Black” Benjamin, Howard Stern Wack Pack Personality, Dies at 64

By Ryan Lotter | Sinist3rKr3w Media

Ronnie Benjamin, better known to listeners of The Howard Stern Show as “Big Black,” has died at the age of 64.

TMZ reported on October 2 that the New York City Medical Examiner’s Office said Benjamin died from arteriosclerotic cardiovascular disease. The death was classified as natural. The Medical Examiner’s Office did not disclose to TMZ when or where Benjamin died.

Benjamin became known to Stern listeners through his appearances as part of the show's collection of personalities known as the Wack Pack. While he was not a regular presence on every broadcast, his extended conversations with Howard Stern and Robin Quivers made him a recognizable personality to longtime listeners.

A Memorable Voice in the Stern Universe

Benjamin was from the Bronx and attended the Bronx High School of Science. He was also reported to have been a member of Mensa.

He appeared alongside fellow Stern personality Lawrence Coward, known to listeners as “King of All Blacks.” Their personalities and interactions became part of Benjamin's history with the program.

EntertainmentNow reports that Benjamin first began calling into The Howard Stern Show during the mid-1990s and became an official member of the Wack Pack in 2006.

After spending several years away from the program, Benjamin returned to the show in 2011. His renewed appearances eventually led to a program with Coward called Black on Black.

Friend Spoke With Benjamin Weeks Before His Death

Following reports of Benjamin's death, Coward told TMZ that he had spoken with his longtime friend approximately two weeks before Benjamin died.

According to Coward, Benjamin contacted him about possibly participating in an upcoming live YouTube discussion for fans of the Stern show. Coward said Benjamin appeared fine during their conversation and gave no indication that he was experiencing health problems.

Coward told TMZ that when he later attempted to contact Benjamin again, his call went to voicemail. He subsequently tried calling and texting after receiving inquiries from others concerned about Benjamin but did not receive a response.

Remembering Ronnie “Big Black” Benjamin

Benjamin developed a following through candid conversations about his life and his distinctive interactions with Stern, Quivers and other personalities associated with the show.

His death marks the loss of another recognizable personality from the extensive group of callers and guests who became part of The Howard Stern Show over the decades.

Ronnie “Big Black” Benjamin was 64.
